Our Journey & Mission

Designed for Sensation

At CHIMINI, we design sensory objects that combine the clean light of natural soy with custom essential oil formulations.

The Beginning

A Quest for Olfactory Purity

CHIMINI was born out of a desire to create clean-burning home scent products that elevate daily spaces without compromising on health or ecological sustainability. Frustrated by chemical soot and synthetic fragrances, we spent years testing natural botanical waxes and organic essential oil profiles.

We choose to focus strictly on pure soy, botanical oils, and natural cotton wicks. The result is a slow, soot-free burn that gently releases complex scent notes throughout your room.

The Process

Handcrafted in Small Batches

We pour our candles in small batches of no more than twelve at our Southern California atelier. This slow, artisanal method ensures that wax temperatures remain precise and essential oils blend seamlessly, preventing standard tunneling issues during lighting.

From printing labels on sustainable linen stock to packing each box in recycled cotton pouches, our hands are on every detail of the ritual.
